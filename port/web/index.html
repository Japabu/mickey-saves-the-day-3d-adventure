Mickey Saves the Day

Disney's 3D adventure from 2001, running in your browser. Bring your own copy of the game: the German CD image Micky_auf_Ganovenjagd.iso. It stays on this computer: the page keeps a copy in the browser's storage for this site, and your saved games stay there too.

Saved games

    Export a saved game to keep it or send it to someone; an imported game goes into a free slot of the eight. The game itself shows them under Load.

    Esc opens the port's options (volumes, sharpness, full screen). Source on GitHub.
